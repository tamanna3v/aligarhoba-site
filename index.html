আমাদের কথা · About

AOBA সম্পর্কে

আলীগড় ওল্ড বয়েজ অ্যাসোসিয়েশন অব বাংলাদেশ হলো বাংলাদেশে বসবাসরত ভারতের আলীগড় মুসলিম বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়ের প্রাক্তন শিক্ষার্থীদের সংগঠন। সংগঠনটি প্রতি বছর ঢাকায় বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়ের প্রতিষ্ঠাতা স্যার সৈয়দ আহমদ খানের জন্মবার্ষিকী উপলক্ষে স্যার সৈয়দ দিবস উদযাপন করে। ২০২৫ সালের ১৭ অক্টোবর গুলশান ক্লাবে তাঁর ২০৮তম জন্মবার্ষিকী পালিত হয়। সংগঠনের সভাপতি ড. রেজওয়ান হোসেন ভূঁইয়া।

The Aligarh Old Boys' Association of Bangladesh is the association of alumni of Aligarh Muslim University (AMU), India, living in Bangladesh. Press coverage shows that it brings together AMU alumni, academics and well-wishers in Dhaka, most visibly through its yearly Sir Syed Day celebration.

The association marked Sir Syed Day 2024 at a hotel in Dhaka, celebrating the 207th birth anniversary of AMU founder Sir Syed Ahmad Khan. It held Sir Syed Day 2025 on 17 October 2025 at the Gulshan Club, Dhaka, for the 208th birth anniversary. At both events the president, Dr Rejuan Hossain Bhuiyan, urged attendees to uphold the values Sir Syed championed, including education, tolerance and reform.

A 2012 report described the body as the Aligarh Muslim University Old Boys Association, Bangladesh. It said the general secretary at the time, Prof Dr Shah Kausar Mustafa, had vowed to set up a university in Bangladesh modelled on the Sir Syed University of Engineering and Technology.

About the parent institution: Aligarh Muslim University in Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh, India, grew out of a school Sir Syed Ahmad Khan opened in 1875. The school became the Muhammadan Anglo-Oriental College in 1877 and gained university status under the Aligarh Muslim University Act of 1920. Its alumni in Bangladesh include Muhammad Mansur Ali, a former Prime Minister of Bangladesh, and Humayun Rashid Choudhury, a former President of the UN General Assembly.

লক্ষ্য ও উদ্দেশ্য · Aims

আমাদের লক্ষ্য

  • Bring together Aligarh Muslim University alumni living in Bangladesh
  • Keep alive the legacy of Sir Syed Ahmad Khan and the values he championed, including education, tolerance and reform
  • Strengthen fellowship among alumni, academics and well-wishers
  • Support education in Bangladesh, including the goal, voiced in 2012, of founding a university on the model of the Sir Syed University of Engineering and Technology
  • Build academic and cultural ties between Bangladesh and Aligarh Muslim University

কার্যক্রম · Activities

আমরা যা করি

◆

Sir Syed Day celebration

A yearly celebration of the birth anniversary of AMU founder Sir Syed Ahmad Khan. It was held at a Dhaka hotel in 2024 (207th anniversary) and at the Gulshan Club on 17 October 2025 (208th anniversary), with speeches by the president, the general secretary and guest academics.

✦

Higher education initiative

In 2012 the association's leadership announced a plan to establish a university in Bangladesh modelled on the Sir Syed University of Engineering and Technology.

●

Alumni get-togethers

Reunions and social gatherings for Aligs living in Bangladesh and their families.

▲

Seminars and discussions

Talks on education, Sir Syed's ideas and the Aligarh Movement, with academics and guests.

■

Annual general meeting

A yearly meeting of members to review activities and elect the executive committee.
